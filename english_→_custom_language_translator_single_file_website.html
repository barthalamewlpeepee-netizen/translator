English → Your Language (Rule-based)

This page applies your 3-step transform to each English word:

  1. Flip last letter to the front
  2. Flip the middle letter to the front
  3. Insert an "x" in the middle of the word

Example: "Hello" → "oHxlle"
Notes: punctuation is preserved, capitalization is kept (if a word starts with uppercase it will remain capitalized), and all-ALLCAP words stay uppercase.
